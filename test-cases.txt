✅ TC001 - Room can be booked with valid data

Preconditions: 
   Open https://automationintesting.online/
   Rooms are available for booking

Test Steps:
   1.Scroll to “Check Availability & Book Your Stay”
   2.Set today's date in "Check In" (e.g.2025-07-30)
   3.Set the date for the day after tomorrow in "Check Out" (e.g.2025-07-31)
   4.Click on “Check Availability”
   5.Scroll down to “Our Rooms” section
   6.Click “Book now” for the “Single” room
   7.In the “Book This Room” section, click “Today”
   8.Click “Reserve Now”
   9.Fill in form with:
      - Firstname: John
      - Lastname: Doe
      - Email: test@test.com
      - Phone: +38(012)3456789
   10.Click “Reserve Now”

Expected Result:
   - “Booking Confirmed” message appears
   - Correct booked dates are displayed (2025-07-30 – 2025-07-31)
   - “Return home” button is shown
   - Dates are shown as Unavailable on calendar

Затем нажимаем на кнопку <button type="button" id="doReservation" class="btn btn-primary w-100 mb-3">Reserve Now</button>. После заполнения формы нажать кнопку <button type="button" class="btn btn-primary w-100 mb-3">Reserve Now</button>. После успешной резервации появится <h2 class="card-title fs-4 fw-bold mb-3">Booking Confirmed</h2> и <p class="text-center pt-2"><strong>2025-07-31 - 2025-08-01</strong></p>.
